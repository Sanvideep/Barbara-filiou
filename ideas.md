# Varvara Filiou — Website Design Direction

## Three initial stylistic approaches

### Approach 1 — Olympic Noir
**Very Brief Intro:** A cinematic editorial landing page where the drama of black competition floors meets the warmth of champagne-gold detail. It positions Varvara as both elite athlete and generous mentor, using movement, light, and quiet confidence rather than a conventional corporate layout.

**Probability:** 0.073

### Approach 2 — Mediterranean Training Journal
**Very Brief Intro:** A sunlit Athens-inspired direction using mineral white, terracotta, and cobalt accents with candid training details and handwritten annotations. It feels more personal and documentary, like opening a coach’s field journal.

**Probability:** 0.041

### Approach 3 — Kinetic Ribbon Archive
**Very Brief Intro:** A contemporary art direction built from apparatus silhouettes, ribbon paths, and archival typography. The page behaves like a moving exhibition, with the booking invitation always visible through repeated visual anchors.

**Probability:** 0.086

## Selected direction — Olympic Noir

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial luxury with the discipline of Olympic sports photography and the restraint of a fashion campaign. The page should feel like a choreographed sequence: a strong opening pose, a measured transition into proof and services, then a focused invitation to book.

### Core Principles
1. **Movement with control:** Curves, arcs, and cropped apparatus details suggest motion, but the layout remains calm and legible.
2. **Proof before persuasion:** Olympic history, coaching experience, and qualifications create trust before the form asks for time.
3. **Quiet premium contrast:** Near-black, warm ivory, and antique gold create distinction without loud gradients or decorative clutter.
4. **One clear next step:** No navigation bar, no search behavior, and no competing CTAs. Every path leads to Request a booking.

### Color Philosophy
Ink black is the floor, stage, and focus; warm ivory is the breathing room around the story; antique gold is reserved for achievement, apparatus, and the booking action. The palette turns athletic credibility into something tactile and memorable, while keeping copy readable for Instagram visitors arriving from a small screen.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical choreography rather than a centered marketing grid. The hero uses an asymmetric split with the subject on the right and copy anchored low on the left. The following sections alternate between a text-led proof rail and an apparatus image, then resolve into a wide booking panel. The form uses a single-column stack on mobile and a two-column editorial split on desktop, with the question field stretching across both columns.

### Signature Elements
- A thin gold ribbon-line motif that loops through section markers and subtly echoes apparatus movement.
- Small uppercase eyebrow labels with generous tracking, paired with high-contrast serif headlines.
- A recurring circular apparatus seal: a thin ring, four-point star, and micro-label such as OLYMPIAN / COACH / CHOREOGRAPHER.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a held pose: deliberate, tactile, and reassuring. Buttons lift a few pixels and gain a gold edge on hover; service cards reveal a soft line of movement; the form confirms submission inline rather than interrupting the visitor with a disruptive modal. Focus states remain clearly visible and gold-toned.

### Animation
Use entrance reveals only for major sections, with opacity plus a 12–18px upward transform and staggered timing. Ribbon motifs should use very slow, low-amplitude drift rather than flashy loops. Button presses use a brief scale-down and spring-back. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling decorative motion while keeping focus and state transitions functional.

### Typography System
Use **Cormorant Garamond** for display headlines and pull quotes, with **Manrope** for body copy, labels, form text, and buttons. Headlines should use restrained all-caps or title case with generous line height; labels should be 10–12px with 0.16em–0.22em tracking; body copy should stay between 15px and 18px with comfortable measure.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** Varvara Filiou brings Olympic-level rhythmic gymnastics expertise, choreography, and coaching directly to clubs, athletes, and events that want training with artistry and standards.

**Personality adjectives:** Precise. Warm. Unforgettable.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are assured and cinematic. CTAs are direct but welcoming. Microcopy feels personal, specific, and calm—never hype-driven.

Example lines:
- “From the Olympic floor to the next generation.”
- “Tell us what you are preparing for. We will take it from there.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark uses a refined high-contrast serif with the first name set on a slight vertical rise, paired with a custom ribbon-star emblem. The symbol is an antique-gold continuous loop enclosing a four-point star, suggesting apparatus, precision, and an athlete’s signature finish. The mark should appear visibly in the header and as the favicon, never as an incidental tiny icon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Apparatus Gold — #CBA96B.** This is the ownable accent: warmer and more tactile than yellow, quieter than metallic brass, and used only where the brand wants the eye to land.

## Content and interaction commitments

The page has no navigation bar. The first screen communicates who Varvara is, why she is credible, and how to request a booking. The visible page sections are: hero and credentials, services, biography and apparatus, achievements and coaching proof, education and languages, then the booking enquiry. The booking form asks only for **Full name, Country, Club / Organization, Email, and one open question** about what the visitor is interested in or what they would like Varvara to help with. It includes an inline confirmation state after submission.

No testimonials, reviews, ratings, or fabricated social proof will be added. The website will use the supplied reference image as visual inspiration and the generated editorial assets as supporting imagery while keeping the information grounded in the biography provided by the client.
